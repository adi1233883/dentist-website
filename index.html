Patient information

Short answers to the things new patients ask most.

Booking an appointment

Use the form below or call the practice. Tell us your preferred day and we will get back to you to confirm a time. Submitting the form is a request, not a confirmed booking.

What to expect at your appointment

Your dentist will talk through your concerns, look at your teeth and gums and explain any recommendations before anything is done. Bring a list of questions, and let us know about any worries so we can help you feel at ease.

Looking after your teeth at home

Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth daily, limit sugary snacks and drinks, and attend regular check-ups. Your dentist can give advice that suits you.

Urgent dental concerns

If you have pain, swelling or an injury to a tooth, contact the practice as soon as you can to ask what is possible. Availability of urgent appointments is not guaranteed. For severe swelling affecting your breathing or swallowing, or serious facial injury, call 999 or 112 or go to your nearest emergency department.
